## Brief Overview
This repository is for "Project Egress", a video game for submission as a senior capstone project. The game is a 2D platformer inspired by Portal and Splatoon. <br>
It will feature gameplay elements such as paint tools, paints, and a variety of traps to damage the player. "Paint tools" and "paint types" will be our unique twist to the platforming style that will provide different abilities for the player to interact with!<br>

## Gamplay

### Progression
The main aspect of the game's progression is a level-based system focused on progressing through different areas. On top of levels, there will be a sandbox for the player to practice with the tools and other aspects of "Project: Egress."

### Mechanics
The player character will have different paint tools they can use to interact with the 2D environment. The tools will be able to be equipped at any time and will provide a different method of spreadin paint. For example, one could equip the "paint roller" to spread paint underneath their character or use a "paintball gun" to fire a projectile much further away to spread paint. On top of the tools, the paint system itself also provides variability as the paint is not just for aesthetics. The paint will provide unique abilities such as creating a bouncy surface for the player to jump on or a slippery surface for the player to gain some speed. Each ability will be given a different paint color to differentiate them. 

### Challenges
The game will feature no enemies, only obstacles. The main idea is for the player character to have to progress through puzzles with traps along the way. For example, there could be a moving platform the player needs to jump on to avoid dangerous spikes below!

### Theme
The aesthetics of "Project: Egress" will be drier so the player character's action can visibly affect the world. The idea is to have the aesthetics look like a grey facility with only tans, whites, and blacks. The player would then be able to understand what is important as the paint would pop out and indicate how to progress through the game's puzzles. 

### Story
If time permits, a more complex narritive could be created using dialogue from signs and NPCs or environmental details. 

## Goal
Our goal is to produce a 2D, level-based, and puzzle platformer game by June that features the games core mechanics. The theme and story will likely take the backseat while we focus on the base game itself.
